Our Joint Mission

The REEL Kilifi
Project

Restoring Reef Ecosystems for Enhanced Livelihoods is more than a coral restoration project - it's a transformative initiative in Kenya's Indian Ocean waters.

The project unfolds in the 12,000-hectare Kuruwitu coastal co-management area in Kilifi County. This pioneering zone benefits from shared coastal and marine resource management between the government and the local fishing community, making it a beacon of effective collaborative conservation in Kenya.

"A pioneer in effective collaborative conservation in Kenya - where communities and government protect the ocean together."

Our Goal

To develop a sense of ownership in the local community by fostering peer-to-peer learning in the context of community citizen science, and by sharing the results of the project with the immediate and wider community.

The Future

Looking Ahead:
Project Evolution

From Local Success to Regional Model

The overall aim of this collaboration is to strengthen and extend the participatory coral reef restoration models implemented in the Kuruwitu Tengefu Community Protection Area in Kenya, in order to support local communities.

Living Classroom

The Kuruwitu area will serve as a living classroom for training new communities, sharing experiences and best practices across the region.

Adapted Approach

Following a restoration approach adapted to the socio-economic context of local traditional fishing communities - scalable and sustainable.

"This partnership represents more than coral restoration - it's about empowering communities to become the guardians of their own marine heritage, creating a model that can be replicated across Kenya's coast and beyond."
