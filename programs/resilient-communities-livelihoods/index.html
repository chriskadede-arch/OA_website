Resilient Communities and Livelihoods

Strong communities are the engine of lasting conservation.

Strong, resilient communities are essential to successful collaborative management. Oceans Alive works alongside coastal communities to strengthen local leadership, improve livelihoods, empower women and vulnerable households, and build the capacity needed to manage natural resources sustainably.

Through our SHE Programme and community development initiatives, we support women and households to build sustainable enterprises, improve food security and diversify incomes - reducing pressure on vulnerable marine resources while strengthening governance, leadership and climate resilience.

“By combining livelihoods, empowerment, governance and conservation, communities become active partners in protecting their marine environment - not bystanders to it.”

Our Work Includes

  • Women's enterprises & the SHE Programme
  • Trash4Cash waste-to-income initiatives
  • Kitchen gardens & food security
  • Village Conservation Committees & local governance
  • Socio-economic surveys and community mapping
  • Climate resilience & adaptation planning
  • Social safeguarding & participatory planning

The Approach Behind Everything We Do

Collaborative Management in Action

We believe healthy oceans can only be achieved when communities are at the heart of conservation. Collaborative management brings together local communities, government, scientists, businesses and conservation partners to share responsibility for protecting and sustainably managing marine resources. It is the foundation of everything we do.
