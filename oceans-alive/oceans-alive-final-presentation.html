Background

Who We Are

Oceans Alive is a Kenyan NGO supporting sustainable traditional marine fisheries across Kilifi County and the Western Indian Ocean region.

Vision

Thriving coastal communities managing healthy, productive ocean ecosystems for present and future generations.

Mission

To empower coastal communities through community-led conservation, building capacity for sustainable marine resource management and economic resilience.

Approach

Community-driven conservation through 4 pillars: Governance, Environment, Economic, and Social development.

2023

First CMA Plan in Kenya

KBMU supported with Co-Management Area plan + KCWA re-constituted

2024

First BMU Network in Kenya

Kilifi County 18 BMU Network formed - managing 26,500 hectares

KBMU Profile

Kenya's First Co-Management Area

Governance Structure

  • 300 members manage 12,000 hectares
  • Operate on basis of Constitution
  • By-laws regulate fishermen behavior
  • Business plan generates operation funds

Management Tools

  • CMA Management Plan (First in Kenya - 2023)
  • Catch data monitoring ongoing
  • MCS (Monitoring, Control, Surveillance) system
  • 500 households benefiting from sustainable management
07

Kilifi County BMU Network

First County BMU Network in Kenya

Scale & Scope

  • 18 BMUs managing over 26,500 hectares
  • 7,000+ households benefiting
  • Fishery managed by shared stocks
  • 5 Joint Co-Management Areas (JCMA)

Key Activities

  • JCMA Management plans in place
  • Leadership capacity building supported
  • Drag netting equipment swap program
  • Advocacy capacity to restrict trawling zones

09

Permaculture Program

Addressing Food Security

The Challenge

Kilifi coastal fishing communities have the lowest nutrition levels in the region. Climate-smart solutions needed.

Garden Methods Trialled

  • Spiral gardens
  • Sack gardens (best results)
  • Vertical sack gardens
  • Compost making

Impact

  • 135 households adopted kitchen gardens
  • Improved nutrition through green leafy vegetables
  • Supplemental income generation
  • Climate-smart, sustainable food systems
  • Demo farm established for training
