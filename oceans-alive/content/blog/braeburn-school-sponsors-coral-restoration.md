Braeburn Garden Estate School recently sponsored a coral restoration initiative with Oceans Alive at the Kuruwitu Community Conservation Area.

The Braeburn Coral Village adds seven artificial reef structures to the other sponsored coral restoration at Kuruwitu. It is a fantastic way of supporting coral restoration efforts in Kenya and raising awareness within the school's student body.

![A Braeburn Coral Village reef structure covered in growing coral fragments](/images/blog/braeburn-coral-village/reef-structure-with-corals.jpg "One of the seven reef structures in the Braeburn Coral Village.")

## How the Coral Village works

The Braeburn Coral Village uses a nail artificial reef technique, where coral fragments are out-planted directly onto nails, where they grow and thrive.

The coral fragments will become a significant part of the new reef, providing nurseries for juvenile fish, supporting biodiversity, and improving traditional fisheries.

![Coral fragments growing upright on the nails of a reef structure](/images/blog/braeburn-coral-village/corals-growing-on-nails.jpg "Coral fragments out-planted onto the nails, growing into the new reef.")
