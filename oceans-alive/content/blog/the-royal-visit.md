The Kuruwitu Conservation and Welfare Community Based Organisation (Kuruwitu CBO) and partners the Oceans Alive Foundation were honoured to welcome His Majesty King Charles III to their offices at Vipingo Ridge Beach on 2 November 2023.

## A welcome from the community

His Majesty was met by Area Chief Salome Tangai, Kuruwitu CBO co-founders Des Bowden and Dickson Juma, and Kuruwitu CBO Chairwoman Maryam Jabali, to learn more about the community-led marine conservation pioneered at Kuruwitu.

![His Majesty King Charles III with Des and Tilda Bowden of Oceans Alive](/images/blog/royal-visit/king-charles-with-des-and-tilda.jpg "His Majesty King Charles III with Des and Tilda Bowden of Oceans Alive.")

## Touring the work

His Majesty toured the climate-smart kitchen model gardens and saw the positive impact of Oceans Alive's projects for families in the surrounding community.

He also met the Kuruwitu Beach Management Unit and heard about the implementation of the first 120 km² Kuruwitu Collaborative Managed Area. Next in line was the BMU Women's Group, who talked about their Trash4Cash project and some of the challenges they face.

## A coral reef "at his command"

His Majesty then got involved in the ground-breaking coral restoration project at Kuruwitu, sealing his support of the Kuruwitu conservation efforts with the commemoration of a coral restoration structure.

![His Majesty King Charles III cements a coral plug into a restoration structure](/images/blog/royal-visit/king-charles-cementing-coral-plug.jpg "His Majesty cements a coral plug into the reef structure.")

King Charles III cemented a plug into the structure before it was taken to the sea and launched "at his command" to its permanent position within the Community Conservation Area, initiating the Commonwealth coral village. The reef structure holds 40 coral plugs, which will grow over time to become an important part of the newly restored reef.

## Stories from the elders

Finally, he sat with the village elders and heard their stories of age-old traditional conservation measures, and how these shaped the belief that if one community joins together with a common approach, it can achieve the impossible.

> "We are delighted and proud that His Majesty King Charles III has seen for himself the community efforts for marine conservation in Kenya. He has seen what can be achieved when a small coastal community gets together and acts on the challenges they face. This has developed into a model, a living classroom to positively impact and inspire people, locally, nationally, and across the world."
>
> — Des Bowden, CEO, Oceans Alive Foundation and Patron of the Kuruwitu Conservation and Welfare CBO
