The year 2025 already looks promising!

## Communities coming together for the reef

Despite the threats of climate change and overfishing we encountered in 2024, the local community around Kuruwitu is working tirelessly to restore biodiversity and protect their coral reefs. They do this together with Oceans Alive, the Kuruwitu Conservation & Welfare CBO, the Kuruwitu Beach Management Unit, Vipingo Ridge and other stakeholders, and with the support of King Charles III, who visited Kuruwitu in November 2023.

Climate change awareness is on the rise, and communities are coming together to build resilience and promote restoration programs.
