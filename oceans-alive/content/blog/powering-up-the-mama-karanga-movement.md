In Kuruwitu, women are uplifting women, and together they're transforming communities while saving marine life.

## Who are the Mama Karangas?

The Mama Karangas are a fearless group of women fishmongers leading the charge for Kenya's coastline. Not only are they pioneering Trash4Cash recycling efforts, but they're also taking a bold stand against overfishing, refusing to buy or sell undersized fish to help restore ocean health for generations to come.

![Members of the Mama Karanga group gathered together at a meeting](/images/blog/mama-karanga-handcart/mama-karanga-meeting.jpg "Women uplifting women: the Mama Karangas meet in Kuruwitu.")

## A milestone, thanks to Pelorus Foundation

Thanks to the incredible support of Pelorus Foundation, we've taken a massive leap forward in empowering these changemakers.

At their newly built Mama Karanga office, we celebrated a milestone: the handover of a brand new handcart and custom branded t-shirts, symbols of unity, strength, and progress.

![The Mama Karangas celebrate around their new handcart](/images/blog/mama-karanga-handcart/celebrating-the-handcart.jpg "Celebrating the handover of the new handcart.")

## More than wheels: freedom

But this cart is more than just wheels, it's freedom! The Mama Karangas collect and transport trash, turning waste into opportunity while protecting Kenya's shores.

With the new handcart:

- **Faster** collections
- **Easier** transportation
- **Bigger impact** for their Trash4Cash initiative

Less physical strain means more trash removed, more income earned, and more marine life saved.

> "The Mama Karangas are proof that when women lead, communities thrive and oceans heal."

Together, we're rewriting futures one cartload, one fish, and one shoreline at a time.
