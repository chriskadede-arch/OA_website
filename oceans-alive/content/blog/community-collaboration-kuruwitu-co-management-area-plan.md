Oceans Alive Foundation plays a key role in supporting the local communities in Kuruwitu, Kilifi County, Kenya, to improve their livelihoods. The local community is dominated by fishermen and fishmongers, with approximately 20 community members depending on each fisherfolk.

## Working with the Kuruwitu Beach Management Unit

Oceans Alive works with the Kuruwitu Beach Management Unit and other Community Based Organisations in the Kuruwitu area.

The Kuruwitu Beach Management Unit is a management measure used to bring together fishermen, fishmongers, traders and boat owners working within the Kuruwitu area to promote community management of marine resources.

Oceans Alive supports the Kuruwitu Beach Management Unit (KBMU) through technical guidance and support in the efficient management of their resources. Oceans Alive supported KBMU to conduct the SAGE process and to develop the Kuruwitu Co-Management Area Plan (KCMAP), which sets KBMU as a pioneer and role model to be emulated by other communities.

![A speaker presents to community members and stakeholders at the plan launch](/images/blog/kcmap-launch/kcmap-presentation.jpg "Stakeholders gather for the launch of the Kuruwitu Co-Management Area Plan.")

## The plan is launched

Recently the KCMAP was successfully launched by the Kuruwitu Beach Management Unit, with support from Oceans Alive and funding from BIOPAMA. Stakeholders from different areas graced the occasion to support the plan's action for sustainability.

![The Kuruwitu Co-Management Area Plan tied with an orange ribbon](/images/blog/kcmap-launch/kcmap-document.jpg "The Kuruwitu Co-Management Area Plan, ready for its launch.")

## What the plan does

The Kuruwitu Co-Management Area Plan aims to fulfil the requirements of the Fisheries Management and Development Act (2016). It was developed through a thorough consultative process, with the engagement of various stakeholders.

The KCMAP highlights relevant issues on marine and coastal natural resource co-management. It aims to resolve conflicts in land use, fisheries, forestry, biodiversity, environmental degradation, pollution and community welfare, and proposes a roadmap of management measures and actions to address them.

> "Healthy coastal and marine ecosystems providing sustainable blue economic ecosystem goods and services for dependent communities and stakeholders within the Kuruwitu Co-Management Area."
