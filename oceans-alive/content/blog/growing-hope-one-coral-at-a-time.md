The waves of change are here, and they're carrying something extraordinary: hope for our oceans!

We're thrilled to announce that our second wave of 10 fisher interns has officially joined the reef restoration journey through Project REEL, supported by [Coral Guardian](https://www.coralguardian.org)!

Over the next six transformative months, these passionate ocean advocates will dive deep into the magical world of coral reefs, learning cutting-edge restoration techniques, gaining hands-on experience, and becoming true guardians of the sea.

![Five women fisher interns in wetsuits on the beach](/images/blog/project-reel-second-intern-wave/women-fisher-interns.jpg "Ready to dive in: fisher interns on the beach at Kuruwitu.")

## Why this matters

Coral reefs are the rainforests of the ocean, supporting 25% of marine life while protecting coastlines and sustaining livelihoods. Yet, they're under threat like never before.

That's why initiatives like Project REEL are vital, empowering local fishers with the skills to restore and protect these underwater ecosystems, ensuring a thriving future for marine life and coastal communities.

## What's next?

- **Hands-on training:** from coral propagation to reef monitoring, our interns will master the tools of restoration.
- **Real impact:** every coral fragment planted is a step toward reviving marine biodiversity.
- **Community champions:** these fishers will become ambassadors for sustainable fishing and ocean conservation.

![Two women fisher interns stand back to back in their wetsuits](/images/blog/project-reel-second-intern-wave/two-interns-ready-to-dive.jpg "Six months of training lie ahead for the new interns.")

> "Together, we're not just restoring reefs, we're growing hope, one coral at a time."

The ocean's future starts with us. Let's dive in!
