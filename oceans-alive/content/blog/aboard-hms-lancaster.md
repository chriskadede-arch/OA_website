In July, Oceans Alive were invited, along with the Kuruwitu Beach Management Unit and Kuruwitu CBO, to attend a cocktail party aboard HMS Lancaster, on manoeuvres in the Indian Ocean and docked at Mombasa for a few days.

## A different side of marine protection

It was an amazing experience for Des (CEO, Oceans Alive), Tilda (Director, Oceans Alive), Ali (Chairman, Kuruwitu BMU) and Mariam (Chairlady, Kuruwitu CBO), who saw a totally different aspect of marine protection.

The armoury of the ship is particularly impressive. Tilda was given a tour of the offensive and defensive capacity of the ship, and heard about how the Lancaster led two successful drug busts earlier this year, where over £33 million of drugs were seized at sea.

![The Kuruwitu team aboard HMS Lancaster in front of the ship's crest](/images/blog/hms-lancaster-visit/kuruwitu-team-aboard-hms-lancaster.jpg "The team from Kuruwitu aboard HMS Lancaster at Mombasa.")

## Sharing Kuruwitu's story

Lancaster Commander Chris Sharp and Deputy British High Commissioner Leigh Stubblefield welcomed the team from Kuruwitu, and heard about how Oceans Alive, the Kuruwitu BMU and the Kuruwitu CBO are also working together to protect the sea.
