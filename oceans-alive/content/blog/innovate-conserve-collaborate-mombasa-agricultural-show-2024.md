At the Mombasa Agricultural Show 2024, Oceans Alive proudly showcased several of our innovative projects, focusing on sustainable and environmentally friendly solutions.

## Climate-smart kitchen gardening

We introduced our climate-smart kitchen gardening innovation, which highlights modern techniques for urban farming, allowing communities to grow food sustainably in small spaces while addressing climate challenges.

## Coral restoration on show

Additionally, we shared our coral restoration innovation and techniques, demonstrating our commitment to marine conservation and ecosystem restoration.

![Pupils watch closely as coral restoration blocks are explained at the stand](/images/blog/mombasa-show-2024/pupils-at-coral-display.jpg "Young visitors discover how coral is grown on restoration blocks.")

## Building awareness and partnerships

Throughout the event, we took the opportunity to create awareness about our work, explaining our unique approach to environmental conservation and sustainable development.

![Pupils gather around the Oceans Alive table to read leaflets about our work](/images/blog/mombasa-show-2024/pupils-reading-leaflets.jpg "Pupils explore leaflets and booklets about Oceans Alive's projects.")

We also built valuable connections by networking with numerous like-minded organisations and institutions, opening doors for future collaborations and partnerships.
