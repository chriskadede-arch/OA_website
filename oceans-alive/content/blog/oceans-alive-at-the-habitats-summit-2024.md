Oceans Alive presented at The Habitats Summit, hosted by The Habitats Trust on the outskirts of New Delhi.

## Chosen from around the world

Oceans Alive was one of 40 organisations chosen after a worldwide vetting journey, which looked for action-oriented, community-centred conservation organisations.
