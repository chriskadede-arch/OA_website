## Inspiring the next generation of ocean stewards

At the heart of Kenya's coastal beauty lies Kilifi, a region blessed with vibrant marine life, especially its precious coral reefs. But these underwater ecosystems face growing threats from climate change, pollution, and overfishing. That's why we're taking action to empower the youngest members of the community to become guardians of the ocean.

We're thrilled to introduce the Project REEL education program, an innovative initiative by Oceans Alive Foundation in partnership with [Coral Guardian](https://www.coralguardian.org), bringing coral reef conservation straight to Kilifi's schools!

![Pupils watch an underwater coral reef film projected onto the classroom blackboard](/images/blog/project-reel-schools/reef-film-screening.jpg "A Project REEL film brings the reef into the classroom.")

## Learning through film, fun, and discovery

Through five captivating film episodes, interactive presentations, and hands-on activities, we're making marine conservation exciting and accessible for students.

![An education officer presents an "Importance of Corals" lesson on a laptop to pupils](/images/blog/project-reel-schools/importance-of-corals-lesson.jpg "Interactive presentations explain why corals matter.")

Our dedicated education officers don't just screen the films, they ignite curiosity, lead lively discussions, and encourage students to think critically about their role in protecting the ocean. And to ensure real impact, we conduct pre- and post-surveys, tracking how knowledge and attitudes evolve over time.

![An education officer leads a discussion with pupils seated on the classroom floor](/images/blog/project-reel-schools/education-officer-discussion.jpg "Education officers lead lively discussions after each film.")

> "When children fall in love with the ocean, they grow up to protect it."

## Growing a generation that protects the sea

By fostering a deep connection with marine life at an early age, we're nurturing a generation that values sustainability and takes action to safeguard our seas.

Healthy coral reefs don't just benefit marine species, they support fisheries, tourism, and coastal protection, making them vital for both people and the planet.

![Pupils watch a Project REEL film closely on a laptop screen](/images/blog/project-reel-schools/pupils-watching-closely.jpg "Curiosity is where ocean stewardship begins.")
