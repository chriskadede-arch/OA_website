From 2003 to today: transforming coastal communities through conservation

Kenya's Conservation Pioneers

  • First Locally Managed Marine Area (LMMA) – Kuruwitu Tengefu, established 2003 under traditional law
  • First Co-Management Area (CMA) – Officially endorsed in 2022 under new BMU Regulations
  • First County BMU Network – 17 Beach Management Units united across Kilifi County
  • First IUCN SAGE Analysis – Site Assessment for Governance and Equity

Setting the standard for community-based marine governance in Kenya

Our 4-Pillar Approach

🏛️ Governance

Supporting fisheries communities in community-based natural resource management through Joint and Co-Management Areas, empowering fisher rights and BMU activities.

🌊 Environment

Assisting sustainable management of ecosystems through coral reef restoration, marine protected areas, and catchment-to-reef management plans.

💰 Economic

Supporting income sustainability in fishery value chains, safeguarding traditional livelihoods while offering diversified income opportunities. For every 1 fisher, 20 livelihoods benefit.

👥 Social

Ensuring "do no harm, leave no one behind" by delivering household benefits through education, improved nutrition, and community harmony.

Transforming 7,000+ Lives

Through the Kilifi County BMU Network, 7,000 coastal households are benefiting from improved governance, restored ecosystems, and diversified incomes.

  • Legal recognition and community rights through officially endorsed BMU Network
  • 135% household income improvement from fish spillover effect
  • Sustainable income streams and diversified economic opportunities
  • Education programs, improved nutrition, and social empowerment
