We Don't Just Study Oceans.
We Transform Them.

Since 2003, we've pioneered community-led conservation that puts coastal communities at the heart of ocean recovery.

From establishing Kenya's first Locally Managed Marine Area to building a network of 17 Beach Management Units, we prove that when you empower communities, entire ecosystems flourish.

This isn't charity. It's partnership. It's science. It's revolution.
