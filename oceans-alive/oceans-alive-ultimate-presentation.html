Since 2003, we've pioneered community-led conservation that puts coastal communities at the heart of ocean recovery.

From establishing Kenya's first LMMA to building a network of 17 Beach Management Units, we prove that when you empower communities, entire ecosystems flourish.

For every 1 fisher, 20 livelihoods benefit in the value chain.

THE 4-PILLAR MODEL

Our comprehensive approach to community-led conservation

🏛️
Governance
Supporting fisheries communities in community-based natural resource management through Joint and Co-Management Areas, empowering fisher rights through BMU activities.
🌊
Environment
Assisting sustainable management through coral reef restoration, marine protected areas, and catchment-to-reef management plans.
💰
Economic
Supporting income sustainability in fishery value chains, safeguarding traditional livelihoods while offering diversified income opportunities.
👥
Social
"Do no harm, leave no one behind" - delivering household benefits through education, improved nutrition, and community harmony.
