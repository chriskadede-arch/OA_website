01 / FOUNDATION

Background

Empowering Coastal Communities

Who We Are

Oceans Alive is a Kenyan NGO supporting sustainable traditional marine fisheries across Kilifi County and the Western Indian Ocean region.

Vision

Thriving coastal communities managing healthy, productive ocean ecosystems for present and future generations.

Mission

To empower coastal communities through community-led conservation, building capacity for sustainable marine resource management and economic resilience.

4-Pillar Approach

  • Governance: BMU networks, policy application
  • Environment: Coral restoration, habitat recovery
  • Economic: Sustainable livelihoods, value chains
  • Social: Education, nutrition, community welfare
02 / JOURNEY

Our Journey

20 Years of Community-Led Conservation

2003

KCWA CBO Formed

Founders concerned at overfishing formed Kuruwitu Conservation and Welfare Association (KCWA) CBO to protect local marine resources.

2006

First LMMA in Kenya

Kuruwitu Community Marine Sanctuary (Tengefu) established - 30 hectares protected. Kenya's first community-driven marine protected area.

2016

NGO Registration

Oceans Alive registered as a marine conservation NGO to scale impact beyond Kuruwitu and support neighboring communities.

2023

First CMA Plan in Kenya

Kuruwitu BMU supported with Kenya's first Co-Management Area (CMA) plan under new BMU regulations. KCWA re-constituted.

2024

First County BMU Network

Kilifi County BMU Network officially formed - 17 BMUs united to manage shared marine resources across the county.

08 / NUTRITION

Permaculture Program

Climate-Smart Food Security

The Challenge

Kilifi coastal fishing communities have the lowest nutrition levels in the region. Climate-smart solutions address food security while building resilience.

Methods Trialled

  • Spiral gardens for herbs and vegetables
  • Sack gardens (best results achieved)
  • Vertical sack gardens for space efficiency
  • Compost making and soil improvement
  • Demo farm for hands-on training

Impact & Results

  • 135 households adopted kitchen gardens
  • Green leafy vegetables improving nutrition
  • Supplemental income from surplus produce
  • Climate-smart, water-efficient systems
  • Toolkit and templates published for replication
